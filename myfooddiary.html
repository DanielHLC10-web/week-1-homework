Food guide by Dani

Did you know...

A taco is a traditional Mexican dish made of a small, hand-sized corn or wheat tortilla wrapped around a savory filling. Eaten entirely by hand, they range from simple street food with onions and cilantro to globally popular crisp-shelled variations

Cooking style of tacos

Taco cooking styles vary widely by region and technique, ranging from ffg and vertical-spit roasting to slow braising, deep-frying, and steam-warming. Each method defines the texture and flavor profile of the iconic Mexican street food.
