Safari Tundra Desert

Tundra

A tundra safari is a guided trip into cold, northern lands to see wild animals like polar bears, musk oxen, and reindeer.

Desert

A desert safari is an outdoor adventure tour in a sandy desert region, most famously run in places like Dubai, that features 4x4 dune bashing, camel rides, and traditional camp dinners.
